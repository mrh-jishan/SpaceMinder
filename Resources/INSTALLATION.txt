SpaceMinder — Install and privacy guide

1. Drag SpaceMinder.app into Applications.
2. Open it from Applications.
3. If this is an ad-hoc-signed direct download, Control-click SpaceMinder.app
   and choose Open once. This is macOS Gatekeeper protecting you from
   unnotarized software.

Optional: fuller storage measurements
-------------------------------------
SpaceMinder works without special access for your own Downloads, Desktop,
Trash, developer caches, and selected folders.

To include protected app data in system-wide measurements, use the "Open
Privacy Settings" button in SpaceMinder and enable it under Full Disk Access.
macOS requires you to make this choice after installation; installers and apps
cannot grant Full Disk Access themselves. This protects your Mail, Messages,
browser, and other sensitive data from silently being read by software.

Privacy
-------
SpaceMinder has no account, analytics, or file upload. Duplicate detection,
folder measurements, iCloud local-copy offloading, and cleanup run only on
this Mac.
